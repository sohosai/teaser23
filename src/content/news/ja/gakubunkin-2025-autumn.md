For autumn enrollments in the English program at the University of Tsukuba.
Your cooperation with payment of the School Festival Student Dues is invaluable.
School Festival Student Dues is the English Translation of GakuensaiGakuseiBuntankin(学園祭学生分担金). Students are asked to pay this fee in order to create a university-wide festival. The school contribution is transferred to the income of the Executive Committee of School Festival every year and is used to cover the costs of running the
festival.

### Amount of the due

The amount of the fee is expected number of years of enrolment × 600 yen paid by new students every year
(bank transfer fees are also to be paid by the individual in the case of payment by bank transfer). In order to
reduce the burden on the Executive Committee, we ask students to pay the amount for the expected years of
enrolment in a lump sum.

### Methods and Deadlines of the collection

#### Programs where collections are made in person

・Japan-Expert (bachelor degree) Program **2 October** (At the First Year Seminar)  
・Bachelor’s Program in Global Issues **14 October** (At the First Year Seminar)  
・Bachelor's Program in Interdisciplinary Engineering **22 September** 14:00~

#### Programs where dues will be collected by bank-transfer

・Undergraduate degree in International Social Sciences  
・Undergraduate Interdisciplinary Program in Life and Environmental Sciences  
・School of Medicine and Medical Science

Please access the link and follow the instructions in the form to pay

from participating in the School Festival for that year only.

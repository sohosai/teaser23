Sohosai is made possible through the generous support of individuals and organizations. Your donations play an essential role in funding the operation of the festival.<br>
We are pleased to announce that, as in previous years, we are accepting donations for Sohosai. Anyone is welcome to contribute, with donations starting from ¥1,000 (one sponsorship unit).<br>
Your generous contributions will be used to support various aspects of the festival, including publicity, safety measures, and other operational expenses, helping us create an enjoyable and memorable experience for everyone involved.<br>
As a token of our appreciation, we have prepared small gifts for those who make a donation.<br>
We sincerely appreciate your support and cooperation in helping us make Sohosai a success.

### Application period

June 1 – November 8
